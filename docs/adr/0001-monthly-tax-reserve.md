# 0001 - Monthly Tax Reserve

Status: accepted (Jane Founder, 2026-01-15)

Each month, move 25% of net profit into the separate tax savings account so tax bills are never a surprise.

Estimated tax payments already sent during that month count toward the 25%. Do not set them aside twice.

Example: monthly profit is $10,000. 25% is $2,500. If $500 of tax was already paid that month, move $2,000 to the tax savings account.
